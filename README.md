# Plumbing Basics Quiz

A small test site for aspiring plumbers. A person enters their name and email, answers 10 questions one at a time, and sees their score and skill level.

**Live page:** https://amfentertains.github.io/plumber-quiz/

This is a test of the idea (a microsite). It has no custom domain.

## How it works

1. The person enters first name, last name, and email, and confirms the email.
2. They answer 10 questions, one at a time. A Back button lets them change earlier answers.
3. On question 10 they click Submit and see their score and level.
4. They click Finish to save their result. A thank-you message shows.
